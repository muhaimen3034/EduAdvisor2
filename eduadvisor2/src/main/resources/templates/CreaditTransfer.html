Credit Transfer Guidelines

Read these guidelines carefully before submitting your request.

✅

Eligibility Criteria

  • Courses must be from an accredited institution
  • Minimum grade of C (or equivalent 2.0/4.0) required
  • Courses must be equivalent in content (≥ 70% overlap)
  • Credits must have been earned within the last 7 years
  • Maximum 60 credits may be transferred into any program
📅

Timeline

  • Submit requests at least 8 weeks before semester start
  • Initial review: 2–3 business days
  • Department evaluation: up to 3 weeks
  • Final decision notification: within 30 days
  • Appeals must be filed within 14 days of decision
📄

Required Documents

  • Official transcript (sealed envelope)
  • Course syllabus for each transfer course
  • Proof of institutional accreditation
  • Degree certificate or enrollment letter
  • Completed credit transfer application form
⚠️

Important Notes

  • Transfer credits appear as "TC" on your transcript
  • Transfer grades do NOT affect your local GPA
  • Core curriculum courses receive priority evaluation
  • Language courses require additional placement test
  • Graduate-level credits cannot be transferred to undergraduate
🔄

Process Overview

  1. Submit online application with all documents
  2. Registrar's office verifies documents
  3. Department head reviews course equivalencies
  4. Academic committee makes final decision
  5. Student notified via email
  6. Approved credits posted to student record
📞
